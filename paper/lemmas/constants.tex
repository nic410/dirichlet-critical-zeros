\subsection{Numerical constants}

Table~\ref{tab:constants} lists the arithmetic constants that occur in the proofs; Table~\ref{tab:Rw} the Gauss-transfer
constants $\Rw$, $\Cw=\CG\Rw$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:A}); Table~\ref{tab:CTfixed} the sharp constants $\CTp(w)$ at fixed $\eta$
(Proposition~\ref{prop:CTfixed}) with the certified values of $p(C)$; Table~\ref{tab:otherw} other weights, for comparison.
All suprema in $t$ are \emph{essential} suprema (Remark~\ref{rem:essup}). For step weights they are evaluated exactly on the
open intervals between consecutive breakpoints $t\in\{\eta/n,1/n\}$; for the log-smooth weight on a grid in $\log(1/t)$, plus a
proved interpolation allowance (in the certified computation, a second-order bound using that $R_S'$ is Lipschitz between grid points;
see the ancillary \texttt{README.md}).
